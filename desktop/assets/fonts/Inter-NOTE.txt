Inter.ttf is a modified copy of InterVariable.ttf from Inter 4.1
(https://github.com/rsms/inter/releases/tag/v4.1), made by desktop/tools/make-inter.py:
instanced to weights 300-700 at optical size 14, digits 0-9 set to their tabular forms,
unreachable glyphs and hinting removed. It remains under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 in OFL.txt
(the release's LICENSE.txt, unchanged). Copyright (c) 2016 The Inter Project Authors.
