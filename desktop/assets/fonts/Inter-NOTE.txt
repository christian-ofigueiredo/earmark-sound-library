InterVariable.ttf is the release's file, unmodified.

Inter-Numbers.ttf and Inter-Blank.ttf are modified versions of it, made by
desktop/tools/make-inter.py: instanced to weights 300-700 at optical size 14, hinting removed,
the .notdef glyph left empty, and every character removed except, in Inter-Numbers.ttf, the
digits 0-9, which are set to their tabular forms.
